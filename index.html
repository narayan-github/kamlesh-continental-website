Our story

Built by builders,
for the people who build Angul.

Kamlesh Continental opened on 29 June 2014 as the hospitality arm of Kamlesh Construction, a family firm that has been building across Odisha for 35 years. Our founder, Mr. S.B. Swain, spent his career with NALCO and wanted the engineers, auditors and families who visit Angul to have a hotel worthy of the town's ambition.

A decade on, the idea is the same: personal attention, a kitchen people talk about, and a front desk that knows your name by the second night.

Good to know

Before you arrive.

Anything else, ask the front desk. It's open all day and all night.

Ask on WhatsApp

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 12 noon and check-out is by 11 am. If your train or shift runs differently, tell the front desk and we'll do what we can.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Room guests have a complimentary breakfast at the Coffee Shop, which is open 24 hours.

How far are the station and the bus stand?

The New Bus Stand is about 5 minutes away and Angul railway station about 15 minutes (roughly 4 km).

Is there parking and Wi-Fi?

Yes, both are free for guests.

When is the pool open?

Ripples is open from 8 am to 6 pm from February to November, and from 8 am to 5 pm in December and January. There is a separate baby pool.

Can you arrange a car from Bhubaneswar airport?

Ask the front desk when you book and we'll confirm what's available. to confirm

Both. See weddings and celebrations or business and meetings.
