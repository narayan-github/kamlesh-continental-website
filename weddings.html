Wedding FAQ

Before you ask.

A few answers are still with the hotel. Those are marked, and the wedding desk can tell you today.

What is the largest gathering you can host?

More than 1,000 guests, using the lawn and the banquet halls together. On their own, the halls take 100 to 600 guests (individually or combined) and the lawn takes 800.

Is there parking for wedding guests?

The hotel has free on-site parking. How many cars it holds on a wedding day, and whether valet is offered, is to confirm

Can we bring our own decorator or caterer?

The hotel's policy on outside decorators and caterers is to confirm Please ask the wedding desk before you book either.

Until what time can music and the DJ play?

The cut-off time for the halls and for the open-air lawn is to confirm

Can we block rooms for out-of-town family?

Yes. Add the number of rooms and nights in the planner and the wedding desk will reply with availability. Group rates are to confirm

Can we taste the food before we book?

Come for the Sunday buffet at Petals, at lunch or dinner. Private tastings for wedding menus are to confirm

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 12:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Breakfast at the Coffee Shop is included with every room.
