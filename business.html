Good to know

Before you travel.

Need something else? Call the front desk on +91 6764 236281.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 12:00 noon and check-out is by 11:00 am. If you need to arrive early or leave late, ask the front desk and we'll do it when rooms allow.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Room guests get a complimentary breakfast at the Coffee Shop. Petals also serves breakfast from 07:30 to 10:30.

Is there parking and Wi-Fi?

Yes. Parking on site and Wi-Fi are both free for guests.

Can I get a GST invoice in my company's name?

Share your company name and GSTIN when you book or check in and we'll bill it that way. to confirm

How far are the railway station and the bus stand?

The New Bus Stand is about five minutes away. Angul railway station is about 4 km, roughly fifteen minutes by road.

Can you arrange a car from Bhubaneswar airport?

Bhubaneswar is roughly 130 km away, a drive of three to four hours. Ask reservations to arrange a car. to confirm

Is laundry available?

Yes. There's a laundry service, and charges apply.
